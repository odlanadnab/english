Everyone needs a brief bio in their back pocket.

Your bio is related to your elevator pitch — but it is different. This is your chance to introduce who you are and establish credibility, in 60 seconds or less, in language a diverse audience can follow. Draft it, deliver it against the clock, then assess it.

Jaye Goldstein · Founder to Leader

Hi! I'm Jaye Goldstein. I'm the CEO and Founder of Founder to Leader. I've spent the last 20+ years working at the intersection of education and innovation. I founded a lab at MIT to help engineers communicate effectively, I oversaw education innovation across Harvard, and was on the founding team at Petri, a biotech venture fund.
